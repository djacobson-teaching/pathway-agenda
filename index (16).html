01 Pathway discussion

Two pathways

Pathway 1

Improve Canvas organization and student access

Create a consistent, accessible Canvas experience that helps students find course materials and engage with learning.

  • Standardized module structures
  • Embedded activities that guide learning
  • Supported by PD, discussions, and module design
Evidence: in-person observations and Canvas observations
Pathway 1 · Discuss
  1. What should every module have, regardless of subject?
  2. Where do students get lost in Canvas right now?
  3. What does "accessible" look like for a teacher with 20 minutes to fix a course?
Pathway 2

Build meaningful, responsible AI use and digital citizenship

Support teachers and students in using AI and other technology meaningfully and responsibly.

  • Structured digital citizenship discussions
  • AI documentation and reflection
  • Modeling and coaching to guide practice
Evidence: in-person and Canvas observations, including structured AI use in instruction or assignments, discussions, and reflections
Pathway 2 · Discuss
  1. What makes AI use "structured" in an assignment?
  2. Where should AI documentation and reflection live in Canvas?
  3. How do we model responsible use in a way teachers can copy tomorrow?

Where they meet: Pathway 1 builds the space students learn in. Pathway 2 shapes how they use AI inside it. Decide together: do teachers choose one pathway, or engage with both?
